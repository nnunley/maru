# A research brief that states an unchecked fact can come back as evidence

**Situation.** Briefing an agent to read the STEPS team's papers on object representation, I listed
"Back to the Future … 31-bit SmallIntegers" as what the Squeak paper covers. That was my recollection,
not something I had read.

**What happened.** The agent searched the text and reported the phrase is not there (31 appears only as
the number of compact classes), and attributed the claim to the user. Had it been less careful, the
claim would have come back as a sourced finding.

**What I do now.** In research briefs, phrase expectations as questions ("does it state the SmallInteger
width?") or mark them "unverified, check", never as descriptions of the source. Treat any agent claim
that matches my own brief word for word as suspect until quoted from the text.
